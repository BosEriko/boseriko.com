# boseriko.com

The personal website and portfolio of Bos Eriko. It keeps itself up to date by
pulling products, projects, blog posts, and resume details straight from GitHub
and dev.to, so nothing ever has to be edited on the site itself.

---

## Products and projects

- Every public repository marked as a product or a project shows up on its own,
  newest first
- Each one gets its own page with a cover image, a write-up, its tech tags, and
  links to the live site and the source
- Browse by technology — topics like React or TypeScript each have a page
  listing everything built with them

## Blog

- Articles written on dev.to appear on the site with their cover image, reading
  time, reactions, and comments
- Posts can be read in full right on the site

## Resume

- A resume built from live data: experience, top personal projects, awards,
  open-source contributions, and skills
- Laid out as A4 pages on screen, and downloadable as a PDF that matches what
  you see

## Around the site

- A Connect page with links to every social profile
- Everything refreshes on its own once a day
